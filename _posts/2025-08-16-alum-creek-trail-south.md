In August, 2025 my father-in-law and I biked the southern-portion of the [Alum Creek Trail](https://columbusrecparks.com/facilities/trails/greenways/alum-creek-trail/) from Groveport, Ohio to Bexley, Ohio and back. The full trail is 24.5-miles long, so on this trip we only rode the lower 10-mile portion. The first 2 miles from Groveport Cruiser Park to Three Creeks Metro Park is actually part of the Blacklick Trail.

This trip was 23.16 miles out-and-back with an elevation gain of 329 ft. We parked at Groveport Cruiser Park: [https://maps.app.goo.gl/zpTtU7SBTrBTscYu7](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zpTtU7SBTrBTscYu7)
